About Me

I help leaders and teams turn AI ambitions into organizational reality.

I advise leadership teams on AI strategy and governance, and work as a fractional Chief AI Officer for companies that want AI to work in everyday work — with people at the center.

I have 25 years of experience with business-critical IT and digital transformation in Denmark, at organizations such as Saxo Bank, Nexi Group (MitID), BEC, TDC NET, Novo Nordisk, Pandora, Bang & Olufsen and DSB. I teach generative AI and AI agents at the IT University of Copenhagen and DIS Study Abroad, and I run tailored AI programs through aitrainer.dk.

I am a partner in TryZone, a network of experienced senior consultants helping leaders and teams make change succeed, and co-founder of BotTellMe, where we build AI solutions and rapid prototypes.
